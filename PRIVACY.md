# Privacy Policy

**Reels Studio**
Last updated 28 August 2026

## The short version

Reels Studio does not collect your personal information, does not require an
account, and does not upload your video anywhere. Your projects and your media
stay on your device.

## What the app touches

**Your photos and videos.** When you import a clip, the app reads it from your
photo library through the system picker, so it only ever sees what you pick. The
imported media is copied into the app's own storage so your project keeps working
if the original is deleted. Nothing is uploaded.

**Your music.** If you add a track from your music library, it is read locally
and mixed at export. DRM-protected tracks — most Apple Music downloads — cannot
be read at all, and the app tells you so rather than failing quietly.

**Your microphone.** Only while you are recording a voiceover, and only after you
grant permission. Recordings are stored with the project on your device.

**Your projects.** Saved as files in the app's own container. Deleting the app
deletes them.

## What is sent off the device

**Crash reports, via Sentry.** When the app crashes it sends a diagnostic report
containing the crash stack, the app version, the OS version and the device model.
It does not include your media, your project contents, your file names, or any
identifier that points back to you. If you would rather send nothing, crash
reporting can be turned off in Settings.

That is the only network request the app makes. There is no analytics SDK, no
advertising SDK, and no third-party tracking of any kind.

## Data you export

When you export a video, the file goes wherever you send it — your photo library,
another app, a share sheet destination. From that point it is governed by
whatever you sent it to, not by this policy.

## Children

Reels Studio is not directed at children and collects no personal information
from anyone, of any age.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published here with a new
date at the top.
