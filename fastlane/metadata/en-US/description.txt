Reels Studio is a vertical video editor for short-form video — trim, layer, and export in the shape your footage is actually going to be watched in.

EDITING
• Trim, split, reorder and reverse clips on a pinch-zoomable timeline
• Layer multiple tracks, with per-clip opacity and transforms
• Fade, dissolve and slide transitions between clips
• Keyframe animation for position, scale, rotation and opacity

LOOK
• Thirteen colour filters, stackable per clip, with adjustable intensity
• Chroma key with a colour you can sample straight off the frame
• Colour LUTs
• Text overlays with your choice of typeface, weight, outline and drop shadow
• Image and sticker overlays, with a visibility window

SOUND
• Import music from your library, or record a voiceover in the app
• Per-clip and per-track volume, fades, crossfades and ducking
• Waveform display, so you can cut to the beat

EXPORT
• Presets for the common vertical formats, plus a custom size
• Target a bitrate or a file size — useful when an upload has a limit
• H.264 or HEVC

Projects save automatically as you work, and reopen exactly as you left them.

Reels Studio is built on kadr, an open-source Swift video framework. No account. No subscription. Nothing leaves your device unless you export it.
